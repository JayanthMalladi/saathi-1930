Source: `data/fraud_corpus.jsonl` (final, 2,190 records: 1,204 YouTube comments, 524 Play Store reviews, 355 Reddit posts, 107 news headlines). Quotes are copied verbatim from the records (checked by script); trimmed with … where long.

Rough keyword counts across the 2,190 records: 82 mention money held or frozen, 78 direct pleas for help, 64 say their own account was frozen or had a lien, 58 mention UTR / acknowledgement fields, 45 say they are still waiting with no update, 42 say police did not help. Median amount mentioned in the first 1,484 records: Rs 9,000. Play Store reviews added little (12 of 524 mention fraud, mostly cashback complaints). Counts are keyword matches, not hand-checked.
